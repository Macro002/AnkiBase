# Features

## Studying Anki decks

![Studying an Anki card](/AnkiBase/media/shot-study.webp)

AnkiBase studies through **Anki's own reviewer**, which runs in AnkiBase's Anki engine (anki-cli, the same core as Anki Desktop). The order of cards, daily new and review limits, learning steps, burying and the intervals shown on the buttons all come straight from Anki. Each answer is a single quick round trip, so large collections stay fast.

Cards render like they do in Anki:

- Your note type's **HTML and CSS** run inside a sandboxed frame with Anki's `.card` and `.nightMode` classes. Light themes use day mode.
- **Images and audio** load from your collection's media folder through short-lived signed links. `[sound:…]` tags become play buttons. Question audio plays automatically and the answer's audio plays on reveal.
- **Cloze**, **type-in answers** (`{{type:…}}`), **MathJax** (`\( … \)`, `\[ … \]`) and **card scripts** are supported.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Space</kbd> / <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Show the answer, then answer **Good** |
| <kbd>1</kbd> – <kbd>4</kbd> | Again · Hard · Good · Easy |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Replay audio |

<Callout type="info">
  Card scripts run in an isolated origin. They can't read your AnkiBase session or call the API.
</Callout>

## Quizlet sets

![Quizlet Learn](/AnkiBase/media/shot-quizlet-learn.webp)

Paste a Quizlet link on **Import** to bring a set in, images included. Every set has:

- **Flashcards** with shuffle, star filters and hints
- **Learn**, which works like Quizlet's: pick a goal, then go round by round from multiple choice to written answers until every term is mastered. **Write** mode starts with written answers.
- **Test** with configurable question types
- **Match**, **Blast** and **Blocks** games

Progress saves as you go. The **Study** tab is a hub of unfinished sessions across Anki and Quizlet.

![Quizlet Match](/AnkiBase/media/shot-quizlet-match.webp)

## AI stories and reading

![Story builder](/AnkiBase/media/shot-story.webp)

On **Story**, choose an Anki deck, a Quizlet set or your own word list:

- **Smart pick** for Anki weights words by card state (learning, young, mature, new).
- **Smart pick** for Quizlet puts starred terms first, then the terms you miss most.

Then choose the language, your CEFR level, the length, a style, and an optional per-paragraph translation. Stories are written by **Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini** using your own API key.

![Reading](/AnkiBase/media/shot-reading.webp)

**Reading** pre-annotates every word of a story, so tapping one shows its dictionary form, part of speech, meaning in context and a grammar note instantly. Highlight any passage and choose **Explain** for a sentence-level breakdown. Words from your cards, including their inflected forms, are highlighted and listed beside the text.

## Search

![Search](/AnkiBase/media/shot-search.webp)

Search reads like a dictionary: pronunciation, senses, gender, plurals and etymology. **In your cards** lists every Anki note and Quizlet term containing the word, with its state, and you can add a new card from a result.

## Stats

![Stats](/AnkiBase/media/shot-stats.webp)

Anki-style statistics for **everything**, **all Anki**, **all Quizlet**, or a single deck or set:

- today's summary and a calendar heatmap
- reviews (count and time) and future due
- card counts, review intervals and card ease
- hourly breakdown and answer buttons
- true retention and cards added
- for Quizlet, the terms you miss most

## Themes and settings

![Themes](/AnkiBase/media/themes.webp)

**Settings → Appearance** has nine built-in themes, custom accent and base colours with a live preview, and theme import/export. Admins can also edit the **server default**. Other sections cover language, your account and password, AI providers and keys, and user management for admins.

## Mobile

![Phones](/AnkiBase/media/phones.webp)

AnkiBase is fully responsive, with a bottom tab bar, safe-area aware layouts and a full-width answer bar.
